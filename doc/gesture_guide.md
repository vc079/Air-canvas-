 🖐️ Gesture Reference Guide

Welcome to the Advanced Air Canvas. This guide maps out the physical hand gestures required to navigate the application's tools. 

> Important:  The application is designed for single-hand use. Bringing a second hand into the camera frame will trigger the Multi-Hand Guard and temporarily pause all drawing inputs.

  Gesture Mapping

 Total Fingers  Active Mode  Action / How to Use 

0 (Fist) Idle / Pause : Halts all tracking. Use this to move your hand across the screen without leaving an ink trail or dragging the canvas. 

1 (Index) : Draw  Move your index finger to draw continuous lines using your currently selected color. 

2 (Index + Middle) Drag Canvas : A green handle will appear between your two fingers. Move your hand to physically grab and shift the entire canvas around the screen. 

3 Fingers - Color Palette : Summons the color menu at the top of the screen. Hover your index finger over a color box to switch your active ink. 

4 Fingers - Snapshot  Hold your hand still. This instantly saves your current masterpiece as a `.png` file to the `/assets/snapshots/` directory. Includes a 3-second cooldown. 

5 (Open Hand) - Wipe Eraser : Converts your entire hand into a bounding-box eraser. Move your open hand over existing ink to wipe it away dynamically. 

 Tips for Best Performance

Lighting: Ensure you are in a well-lit room so the camera can accurately map your hand landmarks.

Proximity: Keep your hand roughly 1.5 to 3 feet away from the webcam for optimal tracking stability.
